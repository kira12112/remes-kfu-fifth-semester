## 2. Ingested Subject Content Review

### A. DM.5 CAR HARD DENT — Caries and Diseases of Hard Dental Tissues
- **Source Books:**
  - `Cariology Kazan final _7b09916b9d11b600cd6546f1f65eeb02.pdf` (112 pages)
  - `k practical handout_7797a08093cd0f21e7e67862a7cbbb0a.pdf` (33 pages)
  - `k Logbook cariology_a7cc31d6f919c918fa51437132bc5de5.pdf` (9 pages)
- **Verified Topics Ingested:**
  - Pre-treatment considerations: Patient personal assessment, medical history, xerostomia risk.
  - Clinical Examination: Visual inspection on clean, dry teeth, contraindication of sharp explorers in non-cavitated lesions, bitewing radiography as gold standard for proximal lesions.
  - Lesion Activity: Active white spot lesion (dull, chalky, rough) vs Arrested caries (shiny, hard, smooth, pigmented).
  - Cavity Preparation Classes I-V: Outline form, resistance & retention forms, removal of decay.
- **Review Finding:** 100% aligned with Kazan Dental Faculty cariology curriculum.

### B. DM.5 PHARM — Pharmacology
- **Source Books:**
  - `DM.5.PHARM.Theoretical_ec9ad3943ab89bede14baa688cbf4c32.pdf` (116 pages, by Prof. Hisham Alshekhepy)
  - `DM.5.PHARM.Practical_4fcdee63caa7c87bf9cd7d54d34aac5a.pdf` (60 pages)
- **Verified Topics Ingested:**
  - General pharmacology: Definitions of pharmacokinetics (ADME) vs pharmacodynamics.
  - Drug nomenclature: Chemical, Generic (INN), and Trade/Proprietary names.
  - Oral bioavailability and hepatic first-pass metabolism.
  - Autonomic nervous system: Adrenergic (sympathomimetics) & cholinergic agents.
- **Review Finding:** Medically accurate; clinical examples tailored for dental local anesthesia and analgesia.

### C. DM.5 PATHOL ANAT — Pathological Anatomy
- **Source Book:** `DM.5.PATHOL ANAT_003e235e595cd4d033ab3bbea5e385f8.pdf` (37 pages)
- **Verified Topics Ingested:**
  - Autoimmune diseases & breakdown of self-tolerance.
  - Gell and Coombs classification of Hypersensitivity (Type I Anaphylactic, Type II Cytotoxic, Type III Immune complex, Type IV Delayed T-cell mediated).
  - Oral manifestations of autoimmune conditions (Pemphigus vulgaris, SLE).
- **Review Finding:** Clinically grounded; distinguishes immunological mediators clearly.

### D. DM.5 PROPAED INT MED — Propaedeutics of Internal Diseases
- **Source Book:** `DM.5.PROPAED INT MED_c6c17c824f21a0086361b062c33bdb00.pdf` (82 pages, KFU Cairo Campus study guide)
